My Journey

From Hospitality Operations to HR.

I understand people not only from an HR perspective, but from the reality of working alongside them.

I began my career on the hospitality floor, where I learned first-hand how people, service standards and operational pressure come together. Working across guest service and food & beverage environments gave me a practical understanding of teamwork, communication and the importance of creating a positive employee experience.

My transition into Human Resources allowed me to bring that operational understanding into the people function. Starting as an HR Intern, I progressed to HR Coordinator within one year and began managing day-to-day HR operations supporting a workforce of more than 200 employees.

Today, my work spans employee lifecycle management, recruitment, workforce planning, payroll coordination, HR reporting, employee relations and UAE HR administration. I enjoy creating structure, solving people-related challenges and making HR processes practical for both employees and management.
